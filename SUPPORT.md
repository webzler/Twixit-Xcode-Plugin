# Twixit support

Twixit makes App Store screenshots on your Mac: a 3D iPhone, iPad or Mac showing your app's capture, with a headline, supporting text and a background, exported at every App Store size and language. It also makes the App Store banners, and AI agents such as the one in Xcode can make the whole set for you.

## Get help

Email [twixit@webzler.com](mailto:twixit@webzler.com). Tell us your macOS and Twixit versions (Twixit › About Twixit) and what happened; a screenshot helps.

Twixit's own help is in **Help › Twixit Help**.

## Common questions

**How do I set up the agent in Xcode?**
Open Twixit › Settings › Automation and click **Set Up Xcode Agent…**. Twixit turns on automation and opens Xcode's plug-in installer; click **Install**. Then ask the agent, for example: *"Make App Store screenshots for this app."* More in the [plug-in's README](README.md).

**The agent says Twixit isn't available.**
Make sure Twixit is installed from the Mac App Store and **Allow automation** is on in Twixit › Settings › Automation. The first time the agent works on a project, Twixit asks to use the project's folder: choose **Allow…**, then **Allow Access**.

**What's in Twixit Free, and what does Twixit Pro add?**
Twixit Free has every editing feature, automation included, with one project and 2 exported screenshots, and no watermark. A free screenshot can be exported again whenever only its text changes. Twixit Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited projects and exports, and App Store banners, shared with your family through Family Sharing.

**I bought Twixit Pro on another Mac.**
Choose **Restore Purchases** in Twixit › Settings › Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

**Refunds**
Purchases are handled by Apple: request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
